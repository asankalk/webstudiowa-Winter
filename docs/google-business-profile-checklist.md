# Google Business Profile checklist

1. Keep the business name consistent with the website and legal trading identity.
2. Use the preferred HTTPS website URL.
3. Keep the public phone number consistent with the contact page.
4. Select accurate service categories; do not add irrelevant categories for keywords.
5. Write concise service descriptions in plain language.
6. Add genuine, current business, project and team photos with permission.
7. Set a routine for responding professionally to reviews.
8. Use posts or updates for genuinely useful announcements, guides or service changes.
9. Link posts to the most relevant service, plugin or Resources page: `/web-design/`, `/website-maintenance/`, `/web-hosting/`, `/plugins/` or `/resources/` as appropriate.
10. Do not keyword-stuff the business name, review requests or service descriptions.
11. Add genuine project, team or finished-website photos where permission allows; avoid stock claims or misleading before-and-after examples.
12. Keep a simple review-response routine: thank genuine reviewers promptly, respond professionally to concerns and never incentivise misleading reviews.

Review the profile quarterly with website contact details, opening hours and service information.
